# Behavioral Profile — Ekam Bhatia

## Overview
Ekam is a **self-directed builder** pattern: someone who learns by doing, documents to consolidate, and applies rigorously in production. He exhibits unusually high independence for an early-career developer — completing a 19-post technical blog while finishing college, launching a production full-stack platform solo, and transitioning from tech support to software/cloud engineering without a formal degree. High detail-orientation and methodical troubleshooting are consistent themes across all roles.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Self-direction | High | Initiates learning without external structure; blog and freelance project are evidence |
| Detail / precision | High | 95% SLA resolution rate, 98% inventory accuracy — consistently precise regardless of domain |
| Resilience / adaptability | High | Multiple industry pivots; builds from scratch in unfamiliar territory |
| Collaboration / communication | Moderate-High | Authored SOPs adopted org-wide at Amazon; mentored through documentation |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Owns things end-to-end:** Designed, built, deployed, and maintains bhangrascape.ca alone — infra, auth, media pipeline, CI/CD
- **Teaches through writing:** 19-post blog makes complex infrastructure topics approachable; natural knowledge-sharer
- **Troubleshoots to root cause:** At Amazon, resolved 1,000+ issues at 95% SLA — not a "ticket-closer" but a root-cause finder

## How Ekam Works Best
- Small-to-mid team where ownership is real and early
- Mentoring or senior presence available — not as a crutch, but to accelerate depth
- Role where JS/TS/React/Node.js is core and cloud/DevOps fluency is a differentiator, not assumed
- Culture that values curiosity and deep technical exploration
- Hybrid or remote with async communication norms

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Limited professional dev experience (< 1 year):** Frame as "stepped into a production engineering role immediately post-graduation and delivered a live platform." Lean on the freelance project and blog as evidence of applied depth.
- **Diploma, not degree:** Magna Cum Laude GPA 3.7 + AZ-900 cert + production work is a stronger signal than an average degree. Acknowledge directly if asked; pivot to output.
- **No production Python/Java/Go depth:** Honest gap — frame JS/TS stack as primary, Python as learnable (college-level + ML tooling familiarity). Don't claim production Go/Java.
- **DevOps roles often gated by seniority:** Target "junior/associate cloud engineer," "platform engineer," or "DevOps engineer I" tiers. Don't apply to "Sr. DevOps" or "Staff SRE."

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a posting mentions these, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "ownership," "end-to-end," "full ownership of the stack"
- "self-starter," "independent," "autonomous"
- "learning culture," "growth mindset," "curiosity"
- "small team," "startup environment," "wear many hats"
- "documentation," "runbooks," "knowledge sharing"
- "production monitoring," "on-call," "incident response"

When a posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "highly structured process," "change control board," "approval-heavy"
- "primarily maintenance," "legacy system support only"
- "10+ years required" without flexibility language
- "bilingual required (French)" — conversational French only

## Management Style Preferences
- Manager who sets direction and trusts execution, not micro-manages implementation
- Code review culture where feedback is specific and educational, not gatekeeping
- Team that ships: regular deployments, not "big bang" releases every 6 months

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Lead with the builder arc — from tech support to full-stack/cloud solo dev. Emphasize ownership of the full bhangrascape.ca lifecycle.
- **CV:** Front-load the freelance project with production metrics. Use blog as evidence of depth without claiming peer-reviewed credentials.
- **Interviews:** STAR examples from Amazon (SLA + runbooks) and bhangrascape.ca (IaC, auth, CI/CD) cover the full behavioral range.
- **Don't overstate:** Don't claim senior cloud architect or production Python depth. Position as "junior/associate who operates above his experience level on the JS/TS/Azure stack."
