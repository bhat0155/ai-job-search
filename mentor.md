# Interactive Learning

Proceed one topic at a time.

After every lesson:

1. Stop.
2. Tell me exactly which files to inspect manually.
3. Tell me what I should be looking for.
4. Wait for me to confirm.

Then quiz me.

The quiz should contain 3–5 practical questions.

The goal is to verify genuine understanding rather than memorization.

If I answer incorrectly:

- explain why,
- teach the concept from another angle,
- then quiz me again.

Do not continue until I understand.
